# Cookie policy

Controller: **Sebastián Zaruha**, Madrid (Spain). Contact: [sebastianzaruha+web@gmail.com](mailto:sebastianzaruha+web@gmail.com)

## What they are

Cookies and similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), store or read information on your device.

## Types we use

1. **Necessary** (no consent required): your cookie choice and, only if you turn them on, the draft and column templates. They are saved in your browser's local storage and not sent to any server.
2. **Analytics (Google Analytics 4):** Google cookies to measure visits. **Only set if you accept.**

## Cookie and storage table

| Name | Owner | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cookie choice (localStorage) | Cuadra (first party) | Remember whether you accept or reject analytics | 12 months |
| Reconciliation draft (localStorage) | Cuadra (first party) | Save your comparison in progress so you can resume it. Only if you turn on "Save draft". | Until you delete it |
| Column templates (localStorage) | Cuadra (first party) | Remember which columns in your files match each field. Only if you turn on "Save column templates". | Until you delete them |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics 4 | Distinguish users (only if you accept) | 2 years (per Google) |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics 4 | Persist session state (only if you accept) | 2 years (per Google) |

Google Analytics may process data outside the EU: see section 8 of the [Privacy policy](#privacidad).

## How to manage them

- In the cookie notice you can choose **Accept**, **Reject** or **Settings**. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings", in the footer of every page. If you withdraw consent, data stops being sent and Google Analytics cookies are deleted.
- Press **"Delete saved data"** to remove the draft and templates from this browser.
- You can also manage or delete cookies and site data from your browser settings.

If you use a shared computer, do not turn on the draft or templates, or delete them when you finish.
